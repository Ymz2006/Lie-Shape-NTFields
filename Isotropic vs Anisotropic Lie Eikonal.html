Neural Eikonal planner · SE(3) rigid bodies · Eikonal-only losses

Isotropic vs anisotropic Lie Eikonal, against the metric baseline

Three travel-time fields trained with nothing but the Eikonal loss, planned with forward MPPI on every held-out start/goal set of the T-, L- and A-shape environments.

The three models

metric_baseline
S · |∇τ| = 1
Isotropic on the 6-D chart gradient, using the combined clearance speed. Original single-route network. TD and normal losses off.
Lie anisotropic
√(S_d²|∇_v τ|² + S_a²|∇_w τ|²) = 1
lie_metric, sqrtN residual. Separate translation and rotation speeds on the body-frame Lie gradient. Two-route network.
Lie isotropic
S_d · √(|∇_v τ|² + |∇_w τ|²) = 1
lie_isotropic. One speed, the translational clearance, on the same Lie gradient. Two-route network.

All three map the residual as 10⁻²·(√N − 1)² with causality weight e−τ/2 and train for 4,000 epochs. Seeds: metric_baseline 5 / 3 / 3, Lie anisotropic 6 / 3 / 3, Lie isotropic 3 / 3 / 3 on T / L / A.

Loss or network?

metric_baseline runs on the original single-route network while both Lie models use the two-route network. Two controls of Lie isotropic separate the pieces: the same loss on the original network, and the same network with the plain chart gradient in place of the Lie gradient.

On the original network, Lie isotropic lands close to metric_baseline in every environment, so most of its lead on L- and A-shape comes from the two-route network. With the network fixed, swapping the Lie gradient for the chart gradient changes little on T and L; on A it lowers the mean and widens the seed spread, though no set is significant with three seeds.

Method

  • Evaluation is analysis/plan_eval.py with forward MPPI and evaluation seed 0, run through analysis/run_plan_suite.sh. Results are in results/compare/plan/<tag>/<set>.json.
  • T-shape (env1) has nine sets: the standard 1k set, two tighter-clearance sets, non-linear, farthest and hard-plus-far pairs, and rotation-heavy, big-angle and translation-only sets. L-shape (env3) and A-shape (env4) have the first five kinds.
  • Run tags: metric_s*, lie_rsqrtN_s*, lieiso_s*, lieiso_orig_s*, lieiso_chart_s*, with an Lshape3d_env3_ or Ashape3d_env4_ prefix on L and A.
  • The other anisotropic Eikonal-only model with all three environments evaluated, the L1 block sum, ties Lie anisotropic on the three-environment average (67.7 vs 66.4) and is not shown.
  • Three seeds per model on L and A is a small sample; treat per-set differences without a bold delta as direction, not proof.
